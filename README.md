## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Aloft** is a captivating cooperative sandbox game developed by Astrolabe Interactive Inc. for Windows. Set against the backdrop of a beautiful floating island, this cozy survival and exploration game invites players to uncover ancient secrets while transforming the sky into their playground. The **latest version** of Aloft provides a **full feature set** that enhances your experience and creativity.

As you embark on your journey, you will gather resources, build your base, and enjoy the company of up to 8 friends. With regularly updated content, you can dive into a world where exploration and creativity go hand in hand.
